The public page keeps its organic-minimal luxury identity — warm-sand ground, one golden-hour coastal photograph in a single rounded card, serif identity block, translucent capsule link buttons and a glass social icon row. Behind a local credential gate at /admin, the strict back-office register continues unchanged: flat neutral surfaces, 1px hairlines, single-digit radii, tabular label/value alignment, one restrained slate accent, a semantic red family for destructive and error states, and a neutral family for everything else. The console now lays its work out one subject per screen behind a persistent, collapsible navigation rail with a burger control, a destination screen header, an overview screen that routes into every section, and a media library of previously uploaded local images. The single dark surface in the whole product is the sign-in gate at the threshold — the console's own near-black ink tier enlarged to full screen — carrying one light-filled primary action and borrowing no mark from any reference product."

# Katy Delma Links — Design System

## Overview

This is a **link-in-bio** system for travel and outdoor creator *Katy Delma*: one
mobile-first, vertically centred card layered over a full-bleed golden-hour
coastal photograph, floating on a warm sand page. The mood is **organic minimal
luxury** — sunlit, editorial, coastal — closer to a slow-travel magazine spread
than to a neon social template.

The audience is a follower arriving from a social bio on a phone, with a single
thumb and roughly four seconds of attention. Everything is therefore arranged as
one calm vertical column with generous breathing room: a circular portrait, a
serif name set in white, one line of bio, four equal capsule buttons, a small
glass social row, and a whisper-quiet footer.

Three materials carry the whole design:

1. **Photograph.** One full-bleed portrait-orientation coastal image fills the
   card edge to edge (sky at the top, shore and warm timber at the bottom). It is
   an imagery slot, self-hosted locally and never hot-linked.
2. **Readability scrim.** A single top-to-bottom dark olive-brown gradient sits
   above the photograph and below the content. It is darkest at the top and at
   the bottom edge and lightest at roughly one third down, so the identity block
   reads against the calm centre of the photo while the footer and avatar edge
   clear the busier sky and shoreline.
3. **Translucent light surfaces.** The four capsule buttons are near-white frosted
   glass with a soft outer shadow and a 1px inner white hairline; the social icons
   are much lighter glass discs. Ink-coloured labels sit on the frosted capsules,
   white text sits directly on the scrim.

The identity block is the only place with strong typographic voice: an elegant
serif display name, white, softly shadowed, with a clean sans bio line beneath it.
Terracotta appears only as the 20px leading line icons inside the buttons. The
card is the whole product; the sand page around it on wide screens is deliberately
plain so the card reads as a single object, like a printed card on a tabletop.

Deliberate brand anchors: **warm sand** page and daylight ground, **ink black**
for all text on light surfaces, **warm terracotta** as the sole accent.

### The admin surface (a strict operations console at `/admin`)

The same product carries a second surface: a **local-only content console** at the
`/admin` route, where the owner edits every part of the page — identity, images,
page typefaces, theme colours, content-link rows and social-link rows — against a
local database on the owner's own machine. It is an operator tool, not a public
page, and it belongs to the same product — but it deliberately does **not** wear
the page's visual system.

The page is warm, photographic and organic. The console is **precise, neutral and
low-chroma**: a strict back-office register ("строгая, профессиональная
админпанель") built from flat surfaces, crisp 1px dividers, tight spacing,
single-digit corner radii and tabular label/value alignment. There is no sand or
cream tinting, no capsule pill, no soft drop shadow, no gradient, no glass and no
playful colour anywhere in `/admin`. The console never borrows the page's
photographic warmth, and the page never borrows the console's grey neutrality. The
console's tokens live in their own namespaces — `admin-*` for structure and
controls, plus the console-only `control-*`, `field-*`, `error` / `destructive-*`,
`badge-*`, `preview-*` and `scroll-*` families, the `nav-*` family for the shell
and the `auth-*` family for the credential gate and the media grid — and never
reuse a public token name.

Three ideas organise the console:

1. **Structure comes from dividers, not elevation.** Every working surface is flat
   `admin-surface` `#FFFFFF` resting on a neutral `admin-ground` `#F4F5F6`,
   separated by 1px `admin-divider` `#E3E6E8` rules. The console chrome has no
   shadow at all and only one tonal step — `admin-surface-sunken` `#F7F8F9` — for
   recessed groups. Depth is not a device here; a rule is.
2. **Hierarchy comes from weight, case, alignment and rules.** Headings are 600,
   labels 500, values 400. Russian labels sit in a fixed `admin-label-column` and
   values align on one vertical line, so label/value pairs can be scanned instead
   of read. There is no uppercase, no tracked lettering and no colour-coded
   section.
3. **One accent, two jobs.** `admin-accent` `#2F4A63`, a restrained slate blue,
   appears exactly twice in the system: the primary save action and the focus
   ring. A semantic red family (`error`, `destructive-*` `#992B22`) is reserved
   exclusively for destructive actions and errors. Everything else is neutral. No
   state — required, invalid, detected, unknown, overridden, saving, saved, failed
   — is ever carried by colour alone.

The console's interface language is **Russian** and the whole register is built
for it: a Cyrillic-safe system stack, label columns sized for Russian word length,
wrapping that never truncates, line heights that survive long compounds, and no
uppercase or tracking treatment that degrades Cyrillic. The page content being
edited stays English. The console makes no claim of accounts, cloud sync,
multi-user collaboration or publishing: it is a single-operator, local,
offline-marked tool, and the permanent local-mode marker in the header states that
in every state, including saving and failure. It does carry exactly one lock — the
local credential gate described below — and that lock guards a file on this
machine, not an identity.

### The sign-in gate (a lock on a local file, not an account)

The one surface a visitor to `/admin` meets before the console exists is the
**sign-in gate**: a full-screen dark surface, a centred single-column form, a
wordmark, two tall quiet fields and one large light-filled submit button reading
«Войти». It is the only dark surface in the whole product, and that is deliberate:
it is the threshold, not the workspace. The console behind it stays light.

It is built from materials the console already owns. The near-black ground is the
console's own ink tier — `admin-inverse-surface` `#15181A`, already used for the
selected fill of preset segments and icon options — enlarged from a control to a
whole screen. The form panel is one luminance step up that same neutral ladder
plus a 1px hairline; there is no gradient, no glow, no glass and no illustration.
The submit control is filled **light** with ink text because on a near-black
ground the slate accent cannot hold a 3:1 boundary as a large fill; the accent's
other job survives on this screen as nothing at all, while the focus ring switches
to the near-white `auth-focus-ring` so it stays visible on the dark. Nothing from
any reference product is reproduced: the wordmark is this product's own name set
in the console sans, there is no logo image, and no third-party mark, glyph or
accent appears anywhere on the screen.

Three honesty constraints shape the surface:

- **No account is implied.** There is no server, no sign-up, no email delivery and
  no second operator. The screen says so in plain Russian, and the address field
  is labelled as the local record it is — a label stored beside the credential on
  this machine, never sent anywhere.
- **«Забыли пароль?» is a real local action, not a dead link.** It opens the
  credential dialog, which names where the credential lives and offers a genuine
  local reset that clears **only** the credential and keeps every piece of
  content. The dialog states plainly that this is possible *because* the tool is
  local — so the reset is a local action on a local file, not identity recovery,
  and no email is sent, checked or expected.
- **A wrong credential reveals nothing.** One generic message covers a wrong
  address, a wrong password and a combination of both; it never says which part
  was wrong, never says whether a record for that address exists, and never clears
  what was typed.

The gate has three screens in one register: the **first-run setup** (address,
password and confirmation, shown only when no credential exists yet), the
**sign-in** above, and the signed-in **access** screen inside the console where the
credential can be changed or signed out of. A successful sign-in, a successful
reset and a sign-out all move focus to the first meaningful element of the screen
that replaces this one, so a keyboard operator is never left at the top of a page
that no longer contains their control.

### One subject per screen (the burger shell)

The second change is structural. The console no longer stacks every group into one
long scrolling form; it lays the work out **по полочкам** — one subject per screen
— behind a persistent navigation rail.

Eight destinations, exactly, in this order and grouped inside the rail:

| Group | Destinations |
| --- | --- |
| Страница | **Обзор**, **Профиль** |
| Оформление | **Медиа**, **Шрифты**, **Цвета** |
| Содержание | **Кнопки-ссылки**, **Соцсети** |
| Инструмент | **Доступ** |

The rail carries the burger control at its top. On wide screens the burger
collapses the rail to a 64px icon rail and back; the collapsed rail keeps every
destination reachable as an icon with a Russian accessible name and a tooltip. On
narrow screens the rail is not in the flow at all: the same burger opens it as an
off-canvas drawer over the content with a scrim, and the drawer is dismissed by the
scrim, by Escape, by the burger, or by choosing a destination.

The shell is **rail + working column + preview**. Because the preview is the reason
the console exists, it is never traded away for navigation: at every width the
rail, the destination screen and the preview are laid out without overlap, without
clipping, and without a single pixel of body-level horizontal scrolling. The
composition is defined once in Layout and never re-litigated per screen.

Two shell-level rules hold across all eight destinations:

- **One save, one document.** Сохранить and Сбросить к значениям по умолчанию live
  in the action bar that belongs to the shell, not to a screen, so the primary
  action and the unsaved-changes indicator are present and identical everywhere.
  Editing on any destination changes the same single page document.
- **Leaving with unsaved changes is guarded.** Switching destination while the
  document differs from the saved state opens the console dialog naming the
  destination being entered, and the operator chooses to save and continue,
  continue without saving, or stay. The rail also marks which destinations hold the
  pending change.
### The preview must be browsable

The console keeps the real page visible while the owner works, and that preview is
a **real, scrollable viewport** — never a clipped fixed-height card:

- It renders the **real public component tree** at a real device width, from the
  top edge of the sand wash down to the bottom caption line, and it scrolls
  through the whole of it with a visible scroll affordance.
- It offers **viewport width presets** — Телефон (390px), Планшет (768px),
  Широкий (1180px) — so the owner can check every layout the page ships in.
- It offers a way to **open the real page in a new tab**.
- It stays **reachable while the owner edits**: on wide screens the preview column
  is sticky beside the working column and never scrolls out of reach; at
  intermediate widths it stacks with a jump-to-preview affordance in every panel
  heading and in the destination screen header; at 390px it simply narrows.
- It is never **clipped by its container** and never **overlaps the editing
  controls**: scrolling is contained inside the viewport, the viewport has
  `min-width: 0`, and nothing inside it is ever scaled or letterboxed.

The navigation rail changes none of this. The preview belongs to the shell, so it
is present on **every** destination screen — Обзор, Профиль, Медиа, Шрифты, Цвета,
Кнопки-ссылки, Соцсети and Доступ alike — and it is laid out beside the rail rather
than under it, so the rail can never cover it and the drawer scrim is the only
thing that may ever pass over it. Collapsing the rail gives the preview its space
back rather than taking it away.

### Visual grounding of the console register

The console's register was read from the reference console **rendered** at 1440×900
and at 390×844 — not from text extraction. Two of its surfaces are reachable without
an account, and they are precisely the ones that define the register: the **sign-in
gate** at the supplied `/admin` URL (which redirects to its login screen) and the
**credential screen** behind that screen's own "forgot password" link. Its measured
geometry is: a near-black ground `#141414`; a centred content column capped at
**480px** with `60px / 20px` stage padding, its content **left-aligned** inside that
column; a brand block **44px** tall sitting **40px** above the form (`37px` at 390);
stacked field wells **20px** apart (`18.5px` at 390), each a **40px** control at
**3px** radius, filled `#222222` inside a 1px `#3C3C3C` boundary, with `15px` inline
padding, a 13px value and a `#EBEBEB` caret; a label line **25px** tall above each
control with a `#DA4B48` required asterisk 5px after the label; one full-width
light-filled submit in `#EBEBEB` with a `#141414` label, 40px tall, 3px radius,
dimming to `#B5B5B5` on hover; one underlined white link; a section heading of
**32px / 500** with a 13px / 20px description line; a fluid base size that reads
13px at 1440 and 12px at 390; 100ms `cubic-bezier(0, 0.2, 0.2, 1)` transitions on
boundaries and fills; a field that answers both hover and focus by strengthening its
boundary to `#8D8D8D` with no ring; and a **single generic wrong-credential sentence**
— "the email or password provided is incorrect" — delivered as a transient
notification while both typed values stay exactly as entered.

Everything in this console's register comes from that evidence: the near-black
threshold surface, the field well identified by its boundary rather than by its fill,
the single light-filled primary with an ink label, the sentence-case label line above
each control, the left-aligned label-above-control form anatomy, the underlined link
row sitting **between the last field and the submit**, single-digit corner radii, the
hairline as the only structural device, and the refusal to reveal which half of a
credential was wrong.

Where the reference conflicts with an accessibility requirement, the requirement
wins and the difference is recorded here rather than hidden. Its **40px** controls
become **44px** and its **3px** control radius folds into the console's 4 / 6 / 8px
scale, so nothing on the gate is smaller than a 44×44 CSS-pixel target; its 13px
base type (12px at 390) stays fixed at the console's 13–15px sizes, because Cyrillic
needs the extra measure and a Russian label must never shrink to fit; its red
required asterisk `#DA4B48` (≈**4.0:1** on its own ground) is lifted to
`auth-required` `#E39A92` (**7.4:1**) so the marker itself clears 4.5:1; its idle
boundary `#3C3C3C` (≈**1.5:1** on its well) is lifted to `auth-field-border`
`#6E7378` (**3.7:1**) so the control's edge clears the 3:1 non-text minimum; and its
boundary-only focus cue (`#3C3C3C` → `#8D8D8D`, ≈**4.8:1**, no ring) is kept **and**
joined by the 2px `auth-focus-ring`, because a boundary change alone cannot be the
sole visible focus indicator.

Three further differences are deliberate and visible:

1. **The gate is a panel, the reference's is a bare column.** The reference centres
   its form on the near-black ground with no card at all; this gate wraps its content
   in the console's own `auth-card`, because the Russian gate carries more than two
   fields — a wordmark line, a title, an explanation, a link row and a local-mode
   note — and those group the way the console groups every surface: one surface closed
   by a 1px hairline.
2. **Errors persist.** The reference reports a wrong credential in an auto-dismissing
   notification; this console keeps one persistent `role="alert"` strip, because a
   single-operator local tool must not lose the reason its last attempt failed.
3. **Hover brightens, it does not dim.** The reference's light primary darkens to
   `#B5B5B5` on hover; this gate brightens to `#FFFFFF` and reserves that dimmed step
   for the disabled state, so a dimmed control on this screen can only mean "not
   available".

**Surfaces of the reference console that could not be reached.** The reference's
signed-in half — its dashboard, its grouped left navigation, its collection list
views, its document editor with its form rows, and its save action area — sits behind
an account. The supplied URL serves the gate to any visitor, the sign-in request from
that gate returned **401**, and no route to the signed-in console is discoverable from
the supplied page. **Nothing in this specification invents what those screens look
like.** The shell, the rail, the collapse and drawer behaviour, the destination screen
header, the overview rows, the media grid and the action bar described below are
derived from (a) the reference's own measured register above — its near-black /
near-white contrast pair, its single-digit radii, its 1px boundary logic, its flat
surfaces, its one light-filled primary, its short `cubic-bezier(0, 0.2, 0.2, 1)`
transitions and its label-above-control form anatomy — and (b) the product
requirements supplied by the owner. They reproduce the reference's **visual system and
structural conventions, never its brand**: no logo, wordmark, monogram, product name,
glyph or proprietary mark of the reference appears anywhere in this specification, and
the console's wordmark is this product's own name set in the console sans.

## Colors

The palette is a warm, low-saturation daylight set: sand grounds, ink text,
frosted white surfaces over photography, and one terracotta accent.

- **Primary — Ink (`#2C2A26`):** Near-black warm ink. All text and icons placed on
  light frosted surfaces use this colour. Against the effective button surface
  (0.84 white over the photo, ≈ `#EFEDEA`) it measures roughly **13:1**.
- **Secondary — Ink Soft (`#4A4640`):** Muted ink for secondary text, hairlines on
  light surfaces, and any metadata that must recede. ≈ **9.4:1** on white.
- **Tertiary — Terracotta (`#936347`):** The single accent. It is used only for
  the leading 20px line icon in each capsule button and for the social hover fill.
  It holds ≈ **5.1:1** against white and against the frosted button surface, so it
  clears both the 3:1 graphic-contrast floor and the 4.5:1 text floor.
- **Neutral — Warm Sand (`#F3EEE4` → `#E6DDCD` → `#DDD2BC`):** The page ground, a
  soft radial daylight wash that is lightest at the top centre and settles into a
  deeper sand toward the lower edge. It is never pure white, so the photographic
  card always separates from the page.
- **Photographic foreground colours:** `on-photo` (`#FFFFFF`) for the display name,
  `on-photo-soft` (`#FFFFFFEB`) for the bio, `on-photo-muted` (`#FFFFFFB8`) for the
  footer. These are always paired with the scrim; they are never placed directly
  on an un-darkened photograph.
- **Surfaces:** `button-surface` (`rgba(255,255,255,0.84)`) and
  `glass-surface` (`rgba(255,255,255,0.16)`) are translucent by design so the photo
  reads faintly through every control. Hover strengthens the button fill to
  `button-surface-hover` (`rgba(255,255,255,0.96)`).
- **Scrim ramp:** `scrim-top` `rgba(40,35,28,0.55)` → `scrim-upper-mid`
  `rgba(40,35,28,0.18)` at 32% → `scrim-lower-mid` `rgba(40,35,28,0.28)` at 62% →
  `scrim-bottom` `rgba(34,29,22,0.68)` at 100%, painted top to bottom.
- **Focus:** a solid white `#FFFFFF` 2px ring, offset 3px, outside every focusable
  element. White is chosen because it is legible on both the frosted capsules and
  the scrim.

Contrast rules: white text over the scrim-darkened photograph must reach **at
least 4.5:1** for the bio and footer, and at least 3:1 for the large display name
(aim 4.5:1 wherever the chosen photo allows). Ink text on the frosted capsules and
the terracotta icon accent both exceed 4.5:1. If a replacement background photo
cannot hold these ratios, deepen the scrim stops rather than changing the text
colours.

### Authentication colours (the one dark surface in a light console)

The sign-in gate is the console's only dark region, and it introduces **no new hue**
— only the console's own neutral ladder read upward from the bottom, plus one
light-red for the destructive action. Every value below is either an existing
console tier at full-screen scale or a step on that same neutral ramp.

- **Ground and panel:** `auth-ground` `#15181A` is the full-screen surface and is
  literally the console's ink tier `admin-inverse-surface` enlarged from a
  segmented-control fill to a whole screen. `auth-card` `#1B1F22` is one luminance
  step up that same ramp, bounded by a 1px `auth-hairline`

  exactly the point: this is layering, not a lift, and the panel is separated by its

  text and measures **15.1:1** on the card — near-white rather than pure white, so

  labels, secondary lines and links; `auth-ink-muted` `#9BA1A6` (**6.4:1**) carries

  ring, which is the same two-layer construction inverted, and not a new device.

  `auth-hairline` box, so the honesty statement sits on the screen as quiet
  structure rather than as a warning.

Contrast rules for the auth surface are identical to the console's: **4.5:1** for
every piece of text, **3:1** for every boundary and the focus ring, and never a
state carried by colour alone. The dark screen is the console's own monochrome
discipline seen from the other side — not a second colour system.

**What was measured on the reference gate, and what was adjusted.** The reference's
ground measures `#141414`; this gate keeps the console's own ink tier `#15181A`, one

## Typography

Two families, each with one job.

- **Display — serif (Georgia / Times New Roman / Songti SC).** The name "Katy
  Delma" only. Weight 600, white, letter-spacing `0.01em`, with a soft dark text
  shadow (`0 2px 14px rgba(28,22,14,0.5)`) that keeps it legible where the scrim
  is lightest. The serif is the entire editorial gesture of the page — it is not
  used for buttons, labels or the bio.
- **UI — system sans (system-ui / Segoe UI / Roboto / Helvetica Neue / Arial).**
  The bio, every button label, and the footer. Clean, neutral, immediate: it keeps
  the frosted capsules feeling functional and never competes with the serif.

Fluid sizing (mobile-first, no viewport-width-only sizing):

| Level | Token size | Rendered range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| display-name | 34px | `clamp(26px, 7.4vw, 34px)` → 26.6px at 360px, 28.9px at 390px, capped 34px from ~460px up |
| body-bio | 15.5px | `clamp(13.5px, 3.6vw, 15.5px)` → 13.5px at 360px, 14px at 390px, 15.5px on desktop |
| button-label | 16px | fixed; never scaled down, so labels stay thumb-readable |
| caption | 11.5px | fixed, tracked `0.02em`, used only for the footer line |

Line heights: bio `1.55` (≈ 21.8px at mobile size) so a three-line bio stays airy;
display name ≈ `1.12`; button label `1.2`; caption `1.2`.

Weight discipline: exactly two weights appear — 600 (display name, button labels)
and 400 (bio, footer). No italics, no uppercase transforms anywhere. Button labels
are sentence case and are centred inside their capsule so the leading icon never
pushes them off-centre.

### Admin typography (one Cyrillic-safe family, three weights)

The console drops the page's serif entirely. Chrome uses one family for everything:
the Cyrillic-safe stack `system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto,
"Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`. Every named face in it carries Cyrillic on the
platform that supplies it, so Russian chrome never falls back to another script or a
visually unrelated substitute. The public page's own typography is untouched — the
serif belongs to the page, not to the tool that edits it.

Hierarchy is carried by **weight, case, alignment and rules**, never by colour:

| Level | Token | Size / weight / line-height | Job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Console title | `admin-title` | 17px / 600 / 1.3 | wordmark and dialog titles |
| Destination title | `screen-title` | 18px / 600 / 1.3 | the screen header's Russian title |
| Panel heading | `admin-heading` | 14px / 600 / 1.35 | one per panel, sentence case |
| Field label | `admin-label` | 13px / 500 / 1.4 | the Russian label of every control |
| Entered value | `admin-input` | 14px / 400 / 1.5 | every input, textarea, select and URL |
| Aligned value | `admin-value` | 14px / 400 / 1.5, `tnum` | read-only values, counts, widths and media counts, tabular figures |
| Rail destination | `nav-item` | 14px / 500 / 1.4 | one destination label in the rail |
| Rail group caption | `nav-group` | 12px / 500 / 1.2 | «Страница», «Оформление», «Содержание», «Инструмент» |
| Screen purpose line | `screen-purpose` | 13px / 400 / 1.45 | one line under the destination title |
| Helper / metadata | `admin-helper` | 12.5px / 400 / 1.45 | hints, filenames, hex strings, counters |

Rules for Russian:

- **No uppercase anywhere in the console**, and no positive letter-spacing on any
  console text (spacing `0`) — tracked uppercase is exactly what makes Cyrillic
  least legible. Case is never a hierarchy device; weight and size are. This holds
  for the rail's group captions (they are sentence case, not small caps) and for
  every destination title.
- **No truncation and no ellipsis.** Every label, value, URL, filename, hex string
  and error message wraps in its own block with `overflow-wrap: anywhere`.
  `white-space: nowrap` is forbidden in the console and `text-overflow: ellipsis`
  is forbidden everywhere.
- **Line heights that survive long words.** Labels 1.4, values 1.5, helpers 1.45 —
  all unitless and all above 1.3, so a long Russian compound ("Отображаемое имя",
  "Фоновая картинка", "Автоматически определять") grows its line instead of
  clipping or colliding with the next control.
- **Label column.** In a label/value row the label column is
  `admin-label-column` (148px) with `admin-label-min` (120px) as its floor, and the
  value column takes the remainder. If the inline space left for the value falls
  under **200px** the pair stacks — label line above the control — instead of
  compressing or breaking the label.
- **Three weights, no more:** 400 (values, helpers), 500 (labels, badges, rail
  destinations), 600 (headings, screen titles, buttons). No italic, no 700. The
  public page keeps its own two-weight discipline (400/600); the 500 tier exists
  only in the console.
- **Row controls flex around text.** Reorder, delete and icon controls keep fixed
  44px boxes while the text column flexes and wraps between them, so a long Russian
  row label grows the row's height rather than overlapping the buttons.
- URLs, hex strings and filenames stay in `admin-input` at 400 weight with
  `overflow-wrap: anywhere`; no monospace family is introduced, so code-like
  strings never look like a different product. Media filenames in the library use
  `admin-helper` at 400 weight for the same reason.

The sign-in gate, the first-run setup, the access screen and the credential dialog

## Layout

A **single centred card** model, mobile-first.

- **Stage:** full-viewport flex container, `min-height: 100%`, content centred on
  both axes, padding `clamp(0px, 4vw, 40px)` — 40px on desktop, ~15.6px at 390px.
  Its background is a radial daylight wash:
  `radial-gradient(120% 80% at 50% 0%, #F3EEE4, #E6DDCD 55%, #DDD2BC)`.
- **Card:** `max-width: 430px` (measured 430px at 1440px, 359px at 390px), width
  `100%`, `min-height: min(900px, 100dvh)`; at `min-width: 600px` a fixed
  `min-height: 860px` keeps the card from stretching absurdly on tall desktop
  viewports. Corners `clamp(0px, 5vw, 30px)` — 30px on desktop, 19.5px at 390px,
  and effectively square on very narrow phones.
- **Card interior:** one flex column, `align-items: center`, gap **18px**, padding
  `clamp(36px, 8vw, 54px)` top, `clamp(20px, 6vw, 30px)` sides, `clamp(26px, 7vw, 38px)`
  bottom. The `min-height` is inherited from the card so the social row can be
  pushed down.
- **Vertical rhythm, top to bottom:** avatar (`margin-top: clamp(6px, 4vw, 26px)`)
  → identity block (`max-width: 320px`, internal gap 8px) → link stack
  (`margin-top: 8px`, gap **14px**) → social row (`margin-top: auto`,
  `padding-top: 22px`, gap **16px**) → footer (`margin-top: 16px`).
- **Spacing scale:** 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 22px for local stacking, with the semantic
  values above for the card's section rhythm. 14px is the button-to-button step and
  18px separates the major blocks; both are consistent at every width.
- **Horizontal safety:** the button column is exactly as wide as the content box
  (312–370px measured), capsule labels use `overflow-wrap: anywhere` and a
  centring flex label so long labels wrap instead of clipping, and no width may
  push the document wider than the viewport. Measured horizontal overflow was
  `false` at both 1440px and 390px.
- **Responsive transformation summary:** the card is fluid below 430px and
  capped at 430px above it; the card corner radius and inner paddings shrink with
  `clamp()` on small screens; the avatar (26vw max 112px), display name (7.4vw max
  34px) and bio (3.6vw max 15.5px) each scale down together, so the composition
  holds at 360, 390, 768 and 1440px without changing its order.
- **Touch targets:** capsule buttons are 56px tall and full width; social discs are
  44×44px with a 16px gap. Nothing interactive is smaller than 44×44 CSS pixels.

### Admin layout (rail + working column + preview)

The console is a **three-part working environment** — a navigation rail, a working
column and a preview column on a neutral ground — that collapses to a single column

- **Touch and focus:** every console control is at least **44×44 CSS pixels** —
  fields, selects, textareas and buttons are 44px tall, icon buttons are exactly

Обзор is a summary, not an editor. It is a single panel of `overview-row` rows — one

the destination's Russian name in `overview-row-label`, a one-line statement of what
is currently configured in `overview-row-value` (for example «Имя, описание и
подпись заполнены», «Загружено 7 изображений, фон выбран», «Шрифт имени:
Джорджия», «Изменено 3 цвета», «4 ссылки, 4 соцсети»), an optional quiet status
badge reading «Заполнено» / «Пусто» / «Есть изменения», and one 44px
`overview-row-action` ghost button «Открыть» that routes into that destination.
Where a section is genuinely empty the value line says what is missing and the same
button reads «Заполнить», so the overview is never a dead end.

The overview's own header carries the page's identity in one line — the display
name, the saved or pending state, and «Открыть страницу в новой вкладке» — and it
never edits anything. Choosing «Открыть» with unsaved changes present is a
destination change and is guarded exactly like a rail click.

### Sign-in, first-run and access layout

The gate is a **centred single-column form on a full-screen dark surface**, and it
is deliberately simpler than the console: no rail, no panels, no preview.

- **Stage:** `auth-page` fills the viewport with `auth-ground` and centres one
  `auth-card` both ways, with `auth-stage-padding` (24px) as its inline minimum so
  the card can never touch the viewport edge. The stage scrolls vertically when the
  content is taller than the viewport — for example when the credential dialog's
  body is long — and never scrolls horizontally.
- **Card:** `auth-card-width` (420px), width `100%`, radius `auth-card` (8px), 1px
  `auth-hairline`, `auth-card-padding` (32px), and `auth-card-gap` (20px) between
  the wordmark block, the title block, the form and the footer row. Below 480px the
  card padding drops to 24px and the card sits flush within the 24px stage padding;
  at 390px the card is 342px wide with no horizontal overflow.
- **Measured against the reference column:** the reference gates a **480px** column
  with `60px / 20px` stage padding, a **44px** brand block sitting **40px** above the
  form (37px at 390), and **20px** between stacked controls (18.5px at 390); its
  content is **left-aligned** inside that column and only the brand block is centred.
  This gate keeps those conventions — a centred brand line, a left-aligned field
  column, one ~20px step between stacked controls (`auth-form-gap` 18px) — and
  narrows the column to 420px so that the 32px card padding still leaves a 356px
  control measure for Russian labels at 390px.
- **Vertical rhythm top to bottom:** wordmark («Katy Delma Links») → the local-mode
  line in `auth-ink-muted` → the screen title → the one-line explanation → the
  fields, separated by `auth-form-gap` (18px) → the link row → the submit → the
  local note box. This is the reference gate's own order read at 1440 and at 390: a
  brand line, then the form, with the credential link **between the last field and
  the submit**, and the submit as the last control before the quiet note. The rhythm
  is generous on purpose: two fields in a lot of air is the whole screen.
- **Fields:** `auth-field-height` (48px), radius 6px, `auth-field-surface`, a 1px
  `auth-field-border`, 12px inline padding and `auth-input` at 15px. A label sits
  4px above its control and one helper or error line 4px below it — the console's
  own field anatomy, raised one step in size.
- **Submit:** full width, `auth-submit-height` (52px), radius 6px, light-filled
  with an ink label. It is the widest and tallest object on the screen; nothing
  competes with it.
- **Required marker:** every field on this screen is required, so each label row
  carries the `auth-required` asterisk glyph 5px after the label — the reference's
  own placement and lead — together with `aria-required`, so requiredness is never
  colour-only.
- **Link row, in the reference's own position:** the «Забыли пароль?» link sits
  between the last field and the submit, aligned with the field column, and is
  underlined on hover and on focus so it is never identified by colour alone. It is
  never a dead link — it opens the credential dialog below.
- **Below the submit:** the local note — a hairline-bounded box stating that the
  credential is stored on this computer, that there is no account, server or email,
  and that the tool never sends anything off the machine. On the first-run screen
  the same slot holds the equivalent note about where the credential will be
  created.
- **Responsive:** the card is fluid below 420px; the title, the submit and the
  fields keep their sizes rather than scaling down, because at 390px a 52px submit
  is still comfortably thumb-sized; the local note wraps to as many lines as the
  Russian sentence needs. There is no horizontal scrolling at 390px and no clipped
  Russian label anywhere on the gate.
- **The access screen lives inside the console**, so it uses the console layout,
  not this one: full rail, full preview, and one panel of credential rows. Only the
  sign-in gate, the first-run setup and the credential dialog use the dark layout.

### Media library layout

The media library sits inside the **Медиа** destination, below the two upload
fields, in the same panel register — one white panel, 1px hairline, 16px padding,
label/value rows.

- **Current image block:** a label/value row per slot. The value shows the slot's
  current image as a thumbnail at `admin-swatch` (44px) — circular for the avatar,
  a 4px rectangle for the background — beside its filename in `media-filename`, or
  the words «Нет изображения» for an empty background slot and «Файл не выбран» for
  an empty avatar slot. The thumbnail is always the truth: it is the file the page
  would render right now, unsaved changes included.
- **Upload control:** the existing upload field, unchanged, directly above the
  library.
- **Library grid:** below the upload row, an `media-grid` recessed surface
  (`admin-surface-sunken`, radius 6px, 12px padding) containing a wrapping grid of
  `media-tile` tiles — a 96px square at radius 6px, `media-grid-gap` 12px apart,
  `minmax(72px, 1fr)` per column so the grid reflows from five columns at the panel
  width down to three at 390px without ever shrinking a tile below 72px or
  producing horizontal scrolling. Each tile holds the image at `object-fit: cover`
  with a 1px `admin-divider` boundary and, beneath or over the image, the filename
  in `media-filename` wrapped with `overflow-wrap: anywhere` (never truncated, never
  ellipsised).
- **Selection:** clicking a tile selects it. Selection is not the assignment: a
  selected tile shows which file you are about to use, and two explicit 44px
  actions on the selection bar — «Назначить аватаром» and «Назначить фоном» —
  assign it to a slot. The selected tile carries `media-tile-selected` fill, a 2px
  `admin-ink` boundary, `aria-pressed="true"` and a check marker; the tile currently
  used by a slot carries `media-tile-current` fill with `admin-inverse-text` and the
  word «На фоне» or «Аватар» in `admin-badge`. Current and selected are therefore
  two different, separately announced facts, and neither is carried by colour alone.
- **Empty library:** with nothing uploaded the grid is replaced by a `media-empty`
  block carrying one 20px image glyph, one `admin-helper` sentence — «Пока нет
  загруженных изображений. Загрузите первое — оно появится здесь и его можно будет
  назначить аватаром или фоном.» — and one 44px secondary button «Загрузить
  изображение» that opens the same file picker. The block disappears the moment the
  first image exists and returns the moment the last one is removed, so the panel is
  never a blank rectangle.
- **Empty background slot:** an unset background is never a black rectangle and
  never a placeholder image. On the page it renders the sand wash exactly as the
  existing upload-field prose requires, and in the library the corresponding slot row
  reads «Нет изображения» beside an empty `preview-ground` well carrying a generic
  image glyph. The avatar slot has no such state on the page — a missing avatar falls
  back to the page's own ring — so its row reads «Файл не выбран» in the same
  position.
- **No deletion from the library in this pass.** The library adds images and assigns
  them; removing an image from disk is a separate, destructive operation and is not
  part of the Медиа screen's action set, so no delete control exists on a tile. This
  is stated in the panel's helper line so the absence reads as a decision rather than
  as a missing button.
### Preview viewport layout

The preview column holds a header row and the viewport itself.

- **Header row:** an `admin-heading` ("Предпросмотр"), the **viewport preset
  switch**, and the "Открыть в новой вкладке" action as a 44px icon button beside a
  labelled secondary button on wide screens. The row wraps rather than clipping,
  and every control in it keeps a 44×44 target. On Обзор, Профиль, Медиа, Шрифты,
  Цвета, Кнопки-ссылки, Соцсети and Доступ this row is identical — the preview does
  not vary by destination.
- **Viewport:** `admin-preview-viewport` is a `preview-ground` well inside a 1px
  `preview-edge` frame, radius 6px, 12px padding, with a height of
  `min(760px, 72dvh)` and `admin-preview-min-height` (600px) as its floor. Inside
  it, a scroll container (`overflow-y: auto; overflow-x: auto; overscroll-behavior:
  contain`) renders the real page at the preset width — `admin-preview-phone`
  (390px), `admin-preview-tablet` (768px) or `admin-preview-wide` (1180px).
- **The viewport scrolls; the page is never clipped.** The rendered page has no
  fixed height: it is the whole public page, from the top edge of the sand wash to
  the footer note, so the owner can scroll from the very top to the very bottom
  including the bottom caption. A styled scrollbar is always present as the visible
  scroll affordance (`scroll-track` / `scroll-thumb` / `scroll-thumb-hover`), the
  track is never hidden, and thin top and bottom edge fades mark the remaining
  content. Scrolling is contained in the viewport and never scrolls the console
  page.
- **Nothing is scaled.** If a preset is wider than the available frame, the frame
  scrolls horizontally within itself; the inner elements keep their real size — the
  avatar, the display name and the bio are never shrunk, transformed or letterboxed.
- **Never clipped, never overlapping, never lost.** The viewport carries
  `min-width: 0` and never produces body-level horizontal scroll; it is laid out to
  the *inline-end* of the rail, so the rail can never sit on top of it at any width;
  it never overlaps the working column or the action bar; and at narrow widths it
  simply narrows while its own internal scrolling keeps the entire page reachable.
  Below 768px, when the rail is a drawer, the drawer and its scrim pass over the
  page while open and the preview is fully restored the moment the drawer closes —
  the preview is never `display: none`, never unmounted and never replaced by a
  summary. Changing a preset keeps the viewport's relative scroll position so the
  owner stays at the place they were inspecting.
- **Reachable from every destination.** Because the preview column belongs to the
  shell, every destination renders with the preview beside it at ≥1120px and with
  the preview stacked after the panels below that. The "К предпросмотру" action in
  every panel heading and in the destination screen header scrolls to it, so a
  destination with a long panel list can still check the page without losing its
  place.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from **photo, scrim and frosted glass**, not from stacked surfaces.
There is only one elevated object on the page, and that is the card itself.

- **Layering order inside the card:** background photograph (bottom) → scrim
  (`position: absolute; inset: 0; z-index: 1`) → content column
  (`position: relative; z-index: 2`). The card sets `overflow: hidden` and
  `isolation: isolate`, so the photo and scrim never bleed past the rounded
  corners and never blend with the sand page behind them.
- **Card shadow on the page:** `0 30px 70px -25px rgba(58,48,33,0.55)` plus
  `0 4px 18px -8px rgba(58,48,33,0.35)` — a wide, low, warm contact shadow that
  makes the card look laid on the sand rather than pasted onto it.
- **Capsule buttons:** `0 10px 24px -14px rgba(40,30,16,0.5)` with an inner hairline
  `inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.5)`. The inset hairline is what makes the
  frosted fill read as a crisp physical pill; on hover the shadow deepens to
  `0 16px 30px -14px rgba(40,30,16,0.6)` and the hairline strengthens to
  `rgba(255,255,255,0.7)`.
- **Avatar:** `0 12px 30px -10px rgba(33,26,16,0.6)` over the ring —
  `3px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.92)` plus a 1px terracotta hairline
  `0 0 0 1px rgba(147,99,71,0.25)`. The portrait is a shallow disc floating just
  above the photograph.
- **Social discs:** no drop shadow at all, only an inner ring
  `inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.35)` and `backdrop-filter: blur(6px)`; on
  hover the disc fills with terracotta and the ring strengthens. Buttons use
  `backdrop-filter: blur(8px) saturate(120%)`.
- **Motion is depth too:** the entrance lift (below) is what first tells the eye
  that the content sits above the photograph.

### Admin elevation: flat, hairline-separated

The console has **no shadow at all** in its own chrome. Its depth is linear and
tonal, and every state change is expressed as a boundary, a fill or a weight
rather than as a lift.

- **Everything is flat.** Panels, header, toolbar, rail, drawer, screen header,
  rows and controls are plain `admin-surface`; the only step *into* a surface is
  `admin-surface-sunken` (`#F7F8F9`), used for icon pickers, colour rows, upload
  rows, typeface groups, media grids, empty states and the preview column. Nesting
  stops at one level: a recessed group contains plain controls, never a third tier.
- **Hairline hierarchy:** `admin-divider` `#E3E6E8` for passive rules,
  `admin-divider-strong` `#C9CFD3` around surfaces and the preview frame,
  `control-border` `#858F96` on interactive controls, and the semantic
  `error-border` `#C0564C` / `success-border` `#8FBFA0` / `warning-border`
  `#D2B476` on status strips. Rules are always exactly 1px and are never doubled up
  with a shadow on the same edge.
- **States replace elevation.** Hover is a fill change (`admin-row-hover`),
  selection is a fill change plus a 2px ink boundary plus a glyph, the current
  destination is a fill change plus a 2px ink bar plus an ink icon plus
  `aria-current`, focus is the accent ring, invalid is a red boundary plus a red
  surface plus an inline message. Nothing lifts, scales, glows or bounces.
- **Sorting moves ink, not shadow.** A row being moved takes the
  `admin-row-picked` fill, a 1px `admin-ink` boundary and its tabular index moves
  with it — no shadow, no `translateY`, no scale. The order is written only on
  save.
- **Banners and inline errors are flat strips**: a 1px matching border and a
  leading glyph, with no elevation, so they read as information rather than as a
  floating toast.
- **The one shadow in `/admin` belongs to the page.** The real public card rendered
  inside the preview viewport keeps the public card shadow
  (`0 30px 70px -25px rgba(58,48,33,0.55)` and `0 4px 18px -8px
  rgba(58,48,33,0.35)`), because it is the actual page. That shadow is the page's,
  not the console's, and no console element ever borrows it.
- **Motion:** short and functional — 100ms for hover fills, 140ms for focus and
  inline-error appearance, 180ms for row moves, banner changes, drawer and rail
  transitions and the preview's colour/typeface update. Nothing loops and nothing
  bounces. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` every console transition and
  reorder animation is removed while all final states remain fully visible, and the
  rail collapse, the drawer slide and the scrim fade become instant.

### Auth depth: dark, flat, luminous

The sign-in gate has **no shadow** either. On a near-black ground a shadow is
invisible, so the dark screen uses the same device the console uses on white: a
boundary plus a luminance step. The `auth-card` sits one step above the
`auth-ground`, separated by a 1px `auth-hairline`, and the field wells sit one step
below the card, separated by their 1px boundary. Nothing floats, nothing glows, and
the scrim behaviour of the dialog is the console's own no-blur backdrop in its dark
value. The only "light" on the screen is the submit button, and it is light because
it is filled with a light surface — not because anything emits.

**The reference's one shadow, deliberately not reproduced.** The reference does carry
a very shallow shadow on its gate — `0 2px 2px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1)`, deepening to
`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)` on hover and focus — and it is left out here on purpose: on a
near-black ground that value is invisible at best and a smear at worst, and the
console's rule is that a *boundary* carries state. The gate's field wells therefore
state idle, hover, focus, invalid and disabled with their boundary, their fill and the
focus ring, and with nothing else.

The dialog on this surface reuses the console's dialog elevation exactly: a full-viewport
backdrop at `nav-scrim` with **no blur**, and a flat `auth-card` panel with no
shadow, radius 8px and `max-width: calc(100vw - 32px)`.

### Shell depth: the rail is a hairline, not a shadow

The navigation rail, the collapsed rail and the drawer are **flat surfaces with a
1px edge**. Neither the collapsed state nor the drawer is signalled by a shadow:
the collapsed rail is simply a narrower white column sharing the same
`admin-divider` edge, and the drawer is a white column above the console's own
scrim — the scrim does the separating, exactly as it does for the console's
dialogs. Turning the rail into a shadowed panel would introduce a depth device the
console does not have, so it is forbidden. The destination screen header is a plain
white strip closed by a 1px rule, identical in treatment to the header and the
toolbar, and it is never sticky-cum-floating.
## Shapes

The shape language is **soft capsule and circle** — nothing on this page has a
sharp corner.

- Capsule link buttons, the social discs and the avatar are fully rounded
  (`9999px` / `50%`). The pill is the page's signature: same height, same radius,
  same frosted fill, four times over.
- The card is a large rounded rectangle, `clamp(0px, 5vw, 30px)`: a soft tablet on
  desktop (30px), tightening to 19.5px at 390px and to square on narrow phones,
  where the card becomes the viewport itself.
- Icons are **line icons**, 20px, drawn at ~1.8px stroke with rounded caps and
  joins — a journal/pen for Travel Blog, a compass for Travel Tips, a tent for
  Hiking Equipment, a camera for Camera Equipment. Social glyphs are monochrome,
  single-colour, ~20px inside their 44px discs. No filled icon buttons, no icon
  containers, no badge shapes.
- The avatar is a perfect circle with an even white ring, never a squircle and
  never a rectangle with rounded corners.
- Hairlines are used instead of borders: a 1px inner white ring on capsules, a
  1px inner white ring on social discs. No solid 1px stroke lies on top of the
  photograph as a divider.

### Admin shapes

The console's shape language is the deliberate opposite: **small, single-digit
radii and rectangles**. The capsule does not exist in `/admin`.

- **Radii are a four-step scale, all single-digit:** `admin-xs` 3px (the toggle knob
  and the smallest inline tags), `admin-sm` 4px (badges, swatches, icon
  buttons, thumbnails, preset segments), `admin-md` 6px (fields, selects,
  textareas, rows, buttons, banners, the preview viewport), `admin-lg` 8px (panels,
  header, toolbar, dialogs). Nothing in the console exceeds 8px, and
  `rounded.full` (9999px) is never used for a button, a control, a badge or a
  message. The auth and shell scales reuse the same six values (`auth-card` and
  `nav-*` panels at 8px, fields, tiles and destination rows at 6px, the burger and
   rail icons at 4px), so the whole product keeps one shape vocabulary.
- **The reference's control radius is 3px** — measured on its own gate's fields, its
  submit and its controls, and already inside this single-digit family. It is
  absorbed into `admin-sm` 4px rather than copied as a fifth value, so the product
  keeps four radii and no control is ever rounder than 8px. Its control heights are
  absorbed upward the same way: its 40px fields and its 40px submit become the
  console's 44px floor, and the gate's 48px field / 52px submit pair is that floor
  raised one step for a screen with two fields and a lot of air.
- **The one circle** is the avatar thumbnail — and the avatar rendered inside the
  preview — because the public avatar is a circle and the preview must tell the
  truth. There is no circular icon button and no capsule button anywhere.
- **Rules are the structural shape:** horizontal 1px `admin-divider` lines between
  label/value pairs, beneath the header, between sections inside a panel, between
  rail groups, between column groups under the header, and above the sticky action
  bar. A rule is never a coloured bar, never a left accent stripe and never a
  decorative underline. The one exception in kind is the **current-destination
  marker**: a 2px `nav-item-current-bar` in `admin-ink` on the leading edge of the
  current rail item. It is 2px rather than 1px and it is ink rather than a divider
  colour, precisely so it reads as a state marker and not as a passive rule — and
  it is never a hue.
- **Every control shares one footprint:** buttons, fields, selects and segmented
  segments are all 44px tall; every icon button is a 44×44 square; the toggle is a
  48×28 rectangle with a 4px radius whose row is the 44px target; a rail
  destination is a 44px-tall row; a media tile is a 96px square; the two auth
  controls are the deliberate exceptions in **size** only (48px field, 52px submit)
  and share the same 6px radius.
- **Messages are strips, not shapes:** banners and inline errors are full-width
  rectangles that wrap long Russian sentences; badges are small 4px-rounded
  rectangles that grow horizontally with their text (`min-width: 0`, no clipping,
  no ellipsis) — a badge is never used for a sentence, and a pill is never used for
  a message. The credential error on the dark gate is the same strip in its dark
  value.
- **Icons** reuse the page's line set at 16–18px with ~1.6px stroke (plus chevron
  up/down, trash, plus, upload, undo, check, alert, external-link, and for the shell
  a burger and the eight destination glyphs — overview grid, profile, image, type,
  palette, link, share, lock), always inline with a label or inside a 44px square
  button — never a filled icon tile, never an emoji, never a decorative
  illustration. The dark gate adds a small set of the same 1.6px-stroke glyphs
  (lock, alert, check, key) drawn in `currentColor`; none of them is a brand mark
  and none is copied from another product.
## Components

### Card

Anatomy: `section` with `overflow: hidden`, `isolation: isolate`, aspect-free but
`max-width: 430px` / `min-height: min(900px, 100dvh)` (860px from 600px up), corner
radius `clamp(0px, 5vw, 30px)`, painted with the full-bleed background photograph
(`background-size: cover; background-position: center`) and the card shadow. The
photo slot is a **single portrait-orientation coastal image** (source reference
measures 1536×2752) that is self-hosted locally; the avatar slot is a **square
creator portrait** (reference 1024×1024). Never stretch, letterbox or tile either.
Both slots are filled from the console's Медиа destination — the background slot may
be empty, in which case the card renders the sand wash instead of a photograph, and
the avatar slot falls back to the page's own ring.

### Scrim

A single absolutely positioned overlay filling the card,
`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(40,35,28,0.55) 0%, rgba(40,35,28,0.18) 32%,
rgba(40,35,28,0.28) 62%, rgba(34,29,22,0.68) 100%)`, `z-index: 1`. It is opaque to
interaction but never blocks it (content sits above at `z-index: 2`). Its job is
readability: dark at the top for the avatar edge, lightest behind the name and bio,
darkening again under the button stack and over the footer. Tune the stops to the
actual photo so white text keeps **≥4.5:1**, and never render the identity block on
the raw photograph. When the background slot is empty the scrim is not rendered at
all — there is no photograph to darken, and the sand wash carries the text at ink
contrast instead.

### Identity block (avatar, display name, bio)

- **Avatar:** circular image, `clamp(94px, 26vw, 112px)` (101px at 390px, 112px at
  1440px), `object-fit: cover`, `border: 3px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.92)`,
  terracotta hairline, soft disc shadow, `margin-top: clamp(6px, 4vw, 26px)`. Alt
  text carries the creator's name.
- **Display name:** `h1`, serif, white, 600 weight, fluid 26–34px, letter-spacing
  `0.01em`, centred, soft dark text shadow. Exactly one line.
- **Bio:** one paragraph, sans, 15.5px (fluid 13.5–15.5px), `line-height: 1.55`,
  `rgba(255,255,255,0.92)`, centred, constrained to `max-width: 320px`. Exactly
  one line of copy, allowed to wrap to three rendered lines at mobile width.
  Block layout: column, gap 8px, centred.

### Link button (capsule)

Anatomy, left to right: 20px terracotta line icon (fixed, `flex: 0 0 auto`) →
12px gap → centred ink label (`flex: 1 1 auto`, `text-align: center`,
`overflow-wrap: anywhere`). A trailing 20px spacer balances the icon so the label
is optically centred. Height 56px (`min-height`), horizontal padding 20px, radius
`9999px`, background `rgba(255,255,255,0.84)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)
saturate(120%)`, ink text at 600 weight / 16px, shadow as in Elevation. The whole
capsule is the link; the stack is a `nav` with gap 14px.

States:

| State | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Default | `rgba(255,255,255,0.84)` fill, soft shadow, 1px inner white hairline |
| Hover | fill `rgba(255,255,255,0.96)`, `translateY(-2px)`, deeper shadow, stronger hairline |
| Pressed / active | `translateY(0)` — the pill settles back onto the photo, fill stays at the hover value |
| Focus-visible | `outline: 2px solid #FFFFFF`, `outline-offset: 3px`, no fill change |
| Long label | wraps inside the capsule via `overflow-wrap: anywhere`; height may grow, label never clips or overlaps the icon |

### Social icon row (Case A — icon row only)

Because this page has non-social content links, the four social profiles render
**only** as a compact glyph row — never as full-width labelled buttons, and never
duplicated at the top of the page. Anatomy: centred flex row, gap 16px,
`margin-top: auto`, `padding-top: 22px`; exactly four 44×44px circular discs, in
order **TikTok, YouTube, X, Instagram**, each with a monochrome white glyph
(TikTok and X as filled marks at ~18–20px, YouTube and Instagram as ~20px outline
marks). Each disc is a link with an accessible name matching its platform; no text
labels are rendered.

States: default glass fill `rgba(255,255,255,0.16)` with a 1px inner white ring;
hover `translateY(-3px)` with the disc filling to terracotta `#936347` and the
glyph staying white; pressed returns to `translateY(0)`; focus-visible uses the
same 2px white ring with 3px offset. Discs are exactly the 44×44px minimum touch

### Admin: inline validation message

Anatomy: a single wrapped block directly beneath the offending control,
`admin-error` typography (12.5px / 500) in `error-text` `#992B22`, prefixed by a
16px alert line glyph from the shared icon set, wrapped with
`overflow-wrap: anywhere` and **never truncated**. It is announced as a live region
(`role="alert"` on first appearance, then re-used), and referenced by the control's
`aria-describedby` while the control also sets `aria-invalid`. The message sits
**in the flow**, directly above the next control, so a panel does not jump when one
appears in the middle of a list. Validation runs on the first blur after an edit,
then re-validates on input so the message clears the moment the value becomes valid;
it never fires on first keystroke and never fires on page load. Entered values are
never cleared, reformatted or reset by an error — the offending value stays exactly
as typed. Error text is never the only signal: the control also switches its
boundary, its fill and its `aria-invalid`, and failures are summarised in the status
banner as well.

This rule holds unchanged on the dark gate, where the same block appears in
`auth-error` typography and `auth-error-text`, under a control that has taken the
`auth-error-surface` fill and the `auth-error-border` boundary.

### Admin: status banner

Anatomy: a full-width flat strip inside a panel or the action bar, radius 6px, 12px
padding, a 1px matching boundary, one 18px leading line glyph, one
`admin-helper`-sized message that wraps to as many lines as the Russian sentence
needs, and — for failures and warnings — one 44px secondary or ghost action on the
trailing edge. It is a strip and never a pill, it has no shadow, it never overlaps
content, and it never auto-dismisses when it carries an error.

| Variant | Surface / text | Leading glyph | Message and action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pending (loading, saving) | `neutral-status-surface` / `neutral-status-text`, boundary `neutral-status-border` | small clock or spinner | "Загрузка данных…" or "Сохранение…" with the form disabled; no action, no dismissal — it is a status, not a notification |
| Success | `success-surface` / `success-text`, boundary `success-border` | check | "Изменения сохранены" — shown **only after the write has actually succeeded**, and it may self-fade after a few seconds because the saved state is also visible in the fields |
| Error | `error-surface` / `error-text`, boundary `error-border` | alert | "Не удалось сохранить изменения. Проверьте подключение к файлу базы и попробуйте снова." plus a 44px secondary retry action "Повторить"; the form keeps every value and stays editable |
| Warning (unsaved) | `warning-surface` / `warning-text`, boundary `warning-border` | alert | "Есть несохранённые изменения." shown while the edited state differs from the saved state; it is a persistent marker, not a toast, and it appears beside the save action as well as before any reset, any destination change or any sign-out |

Announcements: each result is exposed as a live region — `role="status"` for loading,
saving and success, `role="alert"` for failure and for the unsaved warning — so a
keyboard or screen-reader operator learns the outcome without hunting for it. Only
one banner is visible per result; a failure replaces the pending banner rather than
stacking beneath it. The banner's glyph, boundary and text all state the same thing.
The live-region container exists in the DOM from first paint, so a result injected
later is still announced. Retries follow a plain discipline: the first retry fires
immediately on click, the second and third back off at 2s and 4s, and after three
failures the retry action is replaced by "Сообщить об ошибке" with the local error
identifier.

**Against the reference.** The reference reports its own wrong-credential result as a
single generic sentence in a **transient notification** inside a toast container, with
its `role="alert"` live region already present in the DOM before the message is
injected, and with both typed values left exactly as entered. This console keeps the
live region, the one neutral sentence and the untouched values — the honesty
conventions — and deliberately changes the *persistence*: a banner here never
auto-dismisses, because the operator of a local tool may look away while a write or a
credential check runs, and a failure that disappears on its own is a failure the
operator has to reproduce. An error banner is therefore a strip in the flow, and only
a success banner may fade.

- **Title:** "Создайте пароль для студии", with one `auth-helper` explanation that

- **Fields, in order:** "Адрес" (`type="email"`, with the local-label helper),

- **Footer:** the local note box, stating where both facts are stored and that
  nothing leaves the machine.

| State | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Default | three empty fields, submit enabled |
| Invalid on blur | the offending field takes the dark invalid treatment with its own inline message — "Введите адрес", "Пароль должен быть не короче 8 символов", "Пароли не совпадают." — while the other fields keep their values |

### Admin: access screen rows (the "Доступ" destination)

The signed-in access screen lives **inside** the console, so it uses the console's

### Admin: nav group label

Anatomy: one line above each group of destinations, in `nav-group` (12px / 500) and

## Do's and Don'ts

Public page (link-in-bio):

- Do keep the card the only elevated object — one card, one photograph, one scrim.
- Don't add a second content surface, panel, or inner card; the frosted capsules
  are the only controls on the image.
- Do keep white text exclusively on the scrim layer; never place the name, bio or
  footer on an un-darkened photograph. With no background image assigned, use the
  sand wash and its ink text instead of a darkened scrim.
- Do tune the scrim stops to the chosen photograph so bio and footer hold at least
  4.5:1 and the display name at least 3:1.
- Don't use the terracotta accent for large fills, text blocks or the button
  background; it belongs to the 20px line icons and the social hover state only.
- Do keep exactly two weights (600 / 400) and two families (serif display, sans
  UI) across the whole page.
- Don't scale the display name with viewport width alone — use the clamp range so
  it stays between 26px and 34px.
- Do keep the capsules identical in height (56px), radius, fill and padding, and
  keep the leading icon in the same slot on every row, however many rows the studio
  has saved.
- Don't let labels clip: allow wrapping with `overflow-wrap: anywhere` and keep a
  balancing spacer opposite the icon.
- Do keep every interactive target at 44×44 CSS pixels or larger and every
  focus-visible state a solid 2px white outline at 3px offset.
- Do render social profiles only in the compact glyph row, since non-social links
  are present; don't also render them as labelled buttons, and don't grow the disc
  size or spacing to fit more platforms — the row wraps or stays centred instead.
- Don't add decorative borders, drop shadows on the social discs, uppercase
  transforms, italics, or extra icon containers.
- Do honour `prefers-reduced-motion` with an instant, fully visible final state.
- Don't introduce a new hue outside the sand / ink / terracotta / frosted-white
  set, and never pure black or pure cold grey.

Admin surface (`/admin`):

- Do keep the console strictly neutral and low-chroma: white surfaces on a grey
  ground, 1px dividers, single-digit radii, tight spacing — no sand or cream
  tinting, no capsule pills, no drop shadows, no gradients, no glass, no playful
  colour.
- Don't carry hierarchy with colour or elevation: use weight (400 / 500 / 600),
  sentence case, alignment on the single label column and 1px rules between
  label/value pairs.
- Do reserve `admin-accent` `#2F4A63` for exactly two jobs — the primary save
  action and the focus ring — and never introduce a second accent hue anywhere in
  the console. The rail marks the current destination with ink, not with the accent.
- Don't use the red family for anything but destructive actions and errors; never
  colour "Сохранить" with the destructive fill, and never let a destructive control
  sit in the primary slot.
- Do maintain WCAG 2.2 AA: at least 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for interactive
  control boundaries and the focus ring, and never a state carried by colour alone
  — every state pairs a glyph, a word or a boundary with its tint.
- Do keep every console control at least **44×44 CSS pixels** — fields, buttons and
  selects 44px tall, icon buttons 44×44, preset segments 44px tall, rail
  destinations 44px tall, media tiles at least 44×44, a toggle's target its whole
  44px labelled row — with the accent focus ring visible on every focusable
  element, while the real page inside the preview keeps the public white ring.
- Do keep Russian labels wrapping and growing: no ellipsis, no truncation, no
  `white-space: nowrap`, no uppercase, no positive letter-spacing, a 148px label
  column with a 120px floor, and an explicit stack when the value's inline space
  drops under 200px.
- Don't let a control row clip: clusters wrap under their field rather than beside
  it at 390px, and no console width may produce body-level horizontal scrolling.
- Do keep the preview a real browsable viewport over the whole page including the
  bottom caption, with width presets and a visible scroll affordance, never scaled,
  never clipped by its container, never overlapped by the rail or the drawer and
  never overlapping the editing controls, and reachable from every destination at
  every width.
- Don't style the preview with console tokens — inside the frame the public tokens
  rule, including the sand wash, the card shadow and the frosted capsules.
- Do keep the typeface options to system stacks only: no CDN, no webfont files, no
  `@import`, Cyrillic present in every named family, and a default pair that
  reproduces the page's original families exactly.
- Do keep entered values untouched through validation errors, save failures and
  disabled saving states; never clear, reformat or truncate what the operator typed.
- Don't announce success before the write has actually succeeded, and never let a
  failure clear the form.
- Do confirm every destructive action — row delete, delete-all, reset and
  credential reset — in a dialog that names the exact target, with
  `role="alertdialog"`, focus moved in and returned to the trigger afterwards.
- Do show the local/offline marker in every state and never imply cloud sync,
  collaboration, automatic publishing, a server or an account that the tool does
  not have.
- Don't add a third family, a monospace stack or a second accent hue for URLs, hex
  values or filenames; they stay in the console sans at 400 weight.

Authentication surface and navigation shell:

- Do keep the sign-in gate the **only** dark surface in the product, and build it
  from the console's own ink tier and hairline logic — no gradient, no glow, no
  glass, no illustration, no second typeface.
- Don't reproduce any third-party logo, wordmark, brand mark or brand accent on the
  sign-in screen. The wordmark is this product's own name set in the console sans;
  the accent hue does not appear on the dark screen at all.
- Do state plainly on the gate and in the credential dialog that the tool is local —
  no account, no server, no email delivery, no multi-user access — and that the
  credential lives on this machine.
- Don't make "Забыли пароль?" a dead link or an email promise. It opens a real local
  dialog that names where the credential lives and offers a reset that clears **only**
  the credential while keeping every piece of content, and it says that this is
  possible because the tool is local.
- Do answer a wrong credential with one generic message that never distinguishes a
  wrong address from a wrong password and never reveals whether a record exists —
  and never clear, mask or reformat what was typed.
- Don't move focus to the address field on a failed sign-in; leave focus in the
  password field, because moving it would hint at which half was wrong. Do announce
  the failure through a live region that already exists in the DOM.
- Do keep the light-filled, ink-labelled submit as the only primary action on the
  dark screen; don't fill it with the accent, which cannot hold a 3:1 boundary
  against near-black, and don't add a second filled button beside it.
- Do keep the gate, the first-run setup and the credential dialog in one register
  with one shared field anatomy, one 8px card radius and one 52px submit.
- Don't allow paste blocking, a CAPTCHA, a security question or any second factor on
  the credential fields — there is no remote attacker to defend against.
- Do lay the console out one subject per destination behind the rail, keep the rail
  and the shell separate from the editing surfaces, and let the preview, the action
  bar and the unsaved state persist across every destination change.
- Don't let navigation move the work: collapsing the rail must give the preview more
  room, never less, and opening the drawer must never reflow or unmount the page
  behind it.
- Do keep the current destination stated by fill, a 2px ink bar, an ink icon, a
  weight change and `aria-current` together — never by colour, and never by the
  accent.
- Don't hide a destination at any width. The rail, its collapsed form and the drawer
  always contain all eight, with Russian accessible names that survive the collapse.
- Do guard every destination change, and every sign-out, with the unsaved-changes
  dialog that names where the operator is going and offers save, discard and stay.
- Don't discard anything on the operator's behalf: the guard's default focus is on
  "Остаться", and no destination change, sign-out or reset silently loses a value.
- Do keep the burger a real button with `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` and a
  state-dependent Russian name ("Свернуть меню" / "Развернуть меню" / "Открыть
  разделы"), with a 44×44 target and the accent focus ring.
- Don't let the drawer be the only path to a destination and don't let the scrim
  outlive the drawer — it is removed from the DOM when the drawer closes.
- Do keep the media library's two facts separate and announced: which file a slot is
  using now, and which file is selected to be assigned. Assignment happens only on
  the explicit "Назначить аватаром" / "Назначить фоном" actions, never on a tile
  click.
- Don't truncate, ellipsise or hide a filename, and don't let the grid shrink a tile
  below 72px or produce horizontal scrolling at 390px.
- Do keep "Нет изображения" as a real state — the page renders the sand wash rather
  than a black rectangle or a placeholder image — and keep the library's empty state
  in the same panel, never as a dead end.

Grounded in the reference console's reachable surfaces:

- Do borrow the reference's **register** — near-black ground, a field well read by
  its boundary rather than its fill, a label line above each control, left-aligned
  content in a single centred column, single-digit radii, hairline-separated flat
  surfaces, one filled primary per screen — and never its **brand**: no logo,
  wordmark, monogram, product name, glyph or proprietary mark of the reference
  appears anywhere in this product, and this console's wordmark is its own name set
  in the console sans.
- Don't copy the reference's control geometry literally. Its 40px controls and its 3px
  radius are absorbed into a 44px floor and the console's 4 / 6 / 8px scale, because
  every target here must clear 44×44 CSS pixels.
- Do keep the reference's credential link **between the last field and the submit**,
  aligned with the field column and underlined on hover and focus — and never let it
  become a dead link or an email promise.
- Don't invert the reference's light primary into a dimmed hover. Hover brightens to
  `#FFFFFF`; the dimmed light step belongs to the disabled state alone, so lightness
  never carries two meanings on one screen.
- Do keep the reference's single neutral wrong-credential sentence, and keep it
  **persistent** rather than transient: an error the operator can no longer read is an
  error they must reproduce.
- Do keep the reference's boundary-as-state field logic and its 100ms
  `cubic-bezier(0, 0.2, 0.2, 1)` transition timing, and add the 2px focus ring it
  lacks — a boundary change alone is not a sufficient focus indicator.
- Don't carry its shallow `0 2px 2px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1)` field shadow onto a
  near-black ground, and don't reproduce its fluid base size: this console's type is
  fixed in px so a Russian label never shrinks to fit.
- Don't invent the reference's unreachable screens. Its dashboard, navigation, list
  views, editor rows and save area sit behind an account; the shell described here is
  derived from the reference's register and the owner's requirements, and nothing in
  this specification claims to be a copy of a screen that was never seen.
